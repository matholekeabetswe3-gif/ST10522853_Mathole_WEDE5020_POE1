Our History

Kgotso Foundation was founded in 2022 by Ms. Zamo Mabena. After retiring, Ms. Mabena was deeply concerned by the high number of homeless people in her community. That concern became the heart of Kgotso Foundation — an idea that grew into real action.

Since the organization was established, the community has seen positive change with fewer people living on the streets. During her work, Ms. Mabena also noticed that many learners were struggling academically. She identified this as a critical gap and decided Kgotso Foundation would build a bridge to support them.

For the past 5 years, Kgotso Foundation has been committed to uplifting the community of Pretoria through support for the homeless and academic assistance for learners.
