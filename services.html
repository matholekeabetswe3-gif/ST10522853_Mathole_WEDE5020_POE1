Our Services

Kgotso Foundation provides support to improve lives in our community. Below are our main services:

Shelter Support

We provide assistance to homeless people in the Pretoria community. Our shelter support program focuses on providing basic needs and connecting individuals to resources that can help them get back on their feet.

What we offer:

  • Information about accessing shelter and support services
  • Guidance on available resources for homeless individuals
  • Community referrals for food, clothing, and safety

Academic Support

We believe education is the key to a better future. Kgotso Foundation supports learners who are struggling with their academics by providing resources and encouragement based on Ubuntu - I am because we are.

What we offer:

  • Homework club Monday to Thursday
  • Study materials and tutoring
  • Support based on Ubuntu values
